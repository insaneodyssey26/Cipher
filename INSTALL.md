# Installing Cipher

## Before installing — pause Play Protect

Play Protect may block the install. To avoid this:

1. Open the **Play Store**
2. Tap your profile picture, then **Play Protect**
3. Tap the **settings icon** (top right)
4. Turn off **Scan apps with Play Protect**

You can turn it back on after installing.

---

## Install the APK

Download the APK from the [Releases page](https://github.com/insaneodyssey26/Cipher/releases) and install it.

---

## First launch

Open Cipher and go through onboarding. When the SMS permission screen appears, **skip it for now** — you will grant it after the next step.

---

## Enable SMS permission (Android 13+)

Android 13 and above restricts SMS access for sideloaded apps by default. This is a one-time setup.

1. Go to **Settings > Apps > Cipher**
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** (top right)
3. Tap **Allow restricted settings**
4. Open Cipher and grant SMS permission when prompted

---

Cipher uses SMS permission only to read transaction messages locally. Nothing leaves your device.

If you run into any issues, open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/insaneodyssey26/Cipher/issues).
